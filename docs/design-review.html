Colour and type

The tokens come straight from the brief. Contrast was measured, not estimated: every text pair passes 4.5:1. The line colour is too faint to mark the edge of a control, so inputs, switches and the reply box get a stronger edge colour (#4A5261, 3.1:1).

Space Grotesk, headings and numbers
$1,240 / 9 calls

Scale 12, 14, 16, 18, 24, 32, 48, 64. Figures that change use tabular numerals so they don't jump.

Inter, body and controls

Water heater leaking, 412 Alder St

Body 16px on phones, 14px allowed in the dense app panes, line height 1.5. Corners: 10px controls, 12px bubbles, 16px panels.
